What it does

Kotsu keeps track of the small things you want to do regularly, and makes it a bit of a game.

Daily, weekly, monthly and long-term goals

Check a goal off, count up to a target like 8 glasses of water, work through a checklist, or do it on any 3 days of the week. Long-term goals can have a deadline.

Timers and pomodoro

Timed goals have a timer that keeps running when you close the app, with a live count in your notifications. Pomodoro mode splits the time into focus blocks and breaks.

Streaks, XP and badges

Each check-off earns XP. You get a streak freeze for every 7 days in a row, and new colors, app icons and effects as you level up. There are 27 badges to collect.

Habits you want to stop

Add something like "No sugar" and it counts as done every day. Tap it only on a day you slip.

Treats

XP also earns coins. Set your own rewards, like an episode of a show for 30 coins, and buy them when you've earned them.

Two home screen widgets

One lists today's goals so you can check them off from the home screen. The other shows a single goal with buttons for it, like starting its timer.

Stats for each goal

A habit strength score that doesn't drop to zero after one missed day, plus your best streak, time logged, a chart of the last 8 weeks and the notes you wrote.

Vacation mode and skipping

Pause everything until a date, pause one goal, or skip a goal for the day when you're sick. None of it counts against your streak.

Install it

Kotsu isn't on the Play Store yet, so you install it from the APK file.

  1. Download the APKTap Download for Android on your phone.
  2. Open the fileAndroid asks once to allow installing apps from your browser or file manager. Allow it, then tap Install.
  3. Open KotsuAdd your first goal, and add a widget from your home screen if you like.

To update, download the new version and install it over the old one. Your goals and progress stay. Older versions are on the releases page.

Your data stays on your phone

There's no account and no server. Your goals and history are saved on your phone, and you can export a backup file or have Kotsu save one to a folder every day.
