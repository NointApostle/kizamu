What it does

Kizamu keeps track of the habits and small goals you want to keep up, and makes it a bit of a game.

Daily, weekly, monthly and long-term goals

Check a goal off, count up to a target like 8 glasses of water, work through a checklist, or do it on any 3 days of the week. Long-term goals can have a deadline.

Timers and pomodoro

Timed goals have a timer that keeps running when you close the app, with a live count in your notifications. Pomodoro mode splits the time into focus blocks and breaks.

Streaks, XP and badges

Each check-off earns XP. You get a streak freeze for every 7 days in a row, and new colors, app icons and effects as you level up. There are 27 badges to collect.

Habits you want to stop

Add something like "No sugar" and it counts as done every day. Tap it only on a day you slip.

Treats

XP also earns coins. Set your own rewards, like an episode of a show for 30 coins, and buy them when you've earned them.

Two home screen widgets

One lists today's goals so you can check them off from the home screen. The other shows a single goal with buttons for it, like starting its timer.

Stats for each goal

A habit strength score that doesn't drop to zero after one missed day, plus your best streak, time logged, a chart of the last 8 weeks and the notes you wrote.

Vacation mode and skipping

Pause everything until a date, pause one goal, or skip a goal for the day when you're sick. None of it counts against your streak.

A weekly review

See how the week went next to the week before, which goals went well and which ones keep slipping. Get it as a notification every Sunday if you like.

Templates, shortcuts and spreadsheets

Start a goal from a ready-made one, check things off or start a timer by long-pressing the app icon, and export your history as a CSV file.

Questions

Is Kizamu free?

Yes. There are no ads, no subscription and nothing to buy. The coins in the app are earned by checking off goals. If you'd like to chip in anyway, you can support the app by buying me a coffee.

Do I need an account or an internet connection?

No. Kizamu works offline and has no account or server. Your goals and history are saved only on your phone.

Is Kizamu on the Google Play Store?

Not yet. You install it from the APK file on this page. Android asks once for permission to install apps from your browser or file manager.

Can I move my goals to a new phone?

Yes. Export a backup file from Settings, copy it to the new phone, and restore it there. Kizamu can also save a backup to a folder on your phone every day.

Is there an iPhone version?

No, Kizamu is only for Android for now.

Wasn't this app called Kotsu?

Yes. Kizamu was called Kotsu (コツ) until it was renamed, since other apps already used that name. It's the same app: install Kizamu over Kotsu and your goals and progress stay.

Your data stays on your phone

There's no account and no server. Your goals and history are saved on your phone, and you can export a backup file or have Kizamu save one to a folder every day.

刻む

Kizamu (刻む) is Japanese for "to carve" or "to mark off", the way a clock marks time one tick at a time. Each check-off is one more notch. The tagline, kotsukotsu (コツコツ), is the sound of a chisel, and means doing something steadily, little by little.

Kizamu is free, with no ads and no subscription, and it's made by one person. If it helps you keep your habits, you can support it by buying me a coffee.
